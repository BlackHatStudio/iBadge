# Attendi Logo Intro Visual Identity

## Style Prompt

Create a 3-second enterprise logo intro for Attendi: dark, polished, cinematic, and restrained. The logo remains the primary brand signal while motion adds confidence and clarity. Use a deep navy-black canvas, crisp white typography from the supplied logo, saturated blue accents, and subtle cyan light behavior. Avoid generic neon UI, busy particles, distorted logo treatments, or anything that competes with the mark.

## Colors

- Background: `#01050d`
- Deep navy: `#031225`
- Panel navy: `#071a33`
- Logo blue: `#0078ff`
- Cyan highlight: `#19d4ff`
- Foreground white: `#f7fbff`

## Typography

- Primary: use the typography embedded in the supplied logo image.
- Supporting timeline labels: `Aptos`, `Arial`, sans-serif fallback.

## Motion

- Duration: 3.0 seconds at 60fps.
- Logo reveal: controlled scale and opacity entrance.
- Accent motion: blue check glow and light sweep across the mark.
- Final beat: settle into a readable hold, then fade to black for handoff.

## What Not To Do

- Do not render a final MP4 without explicit approval.
- Do not replace or redraw the logo.
- Do not layer another primary visual over the logo.
- Do not use fast jitter, camera shake, or aggressive glitch effects.
- Do not obscure the Attendi name or tagline.
